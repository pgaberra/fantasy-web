Refund policy

Last updated {{ lastUpdated }}

Premium is a monthly subscription. This page explains when you can receive a refund and how to request one. It sits alongside the terms and conditions.

Cancelling is not the same as a refund

You can cancel from your account page at any time. Cancelling stops the next charge, and Premium remains active until the end of the period you already paid for.

A refund returns money you have already paid.

Your 14 day right to change your mind

If the law gives you a 14-day withdrawal right, you can request a refund within that period without giving a reason.

Premium starts when you pay. Where the law allows a withdrawal right to be limited after immediate performance begins, the applicable amount may be reduced for the period already used. Our current policy is to refund the month in full for requests made within 14 days of the first payment.

Outside the 14-day period

We will refund the current month in full if:

  • the service was substantially broken or unavailable for a meaningful part of the month
  • you were charged after cancelling, or charged twice
  • you never intended to subscribe, for example a subscription taken out on your card without your knowledge

Outside those cases, we do not normally refund months that have already been used. Contact us anyway if your situation is unusual and we will review it.

We do not refund because a projection turned out to be wrong. SlapStat provides estimates, not guarantees.

How to ask

Email info@slapstat.com from the address on your account and tell us which charge you mean. An order number is not required, but the receipt can help.

You can also reply to the receipt from Paddle, which can process refunds for the purchase.

How you get the money

Paddle sends refunds to the payment method you used. It may take a few business days for Paddle and additional time for your bank or card issuer to show the refund.

Premium access ends when the refunded month is refunded.

Disputes

Consumers can use the dispute and complaint routes available under applicable law, including the National Board for Consumer Disputes (ARN) in Sweden.

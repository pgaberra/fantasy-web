Premium is a monthly subscription sold through Paddle, our merchant of record. This policy is part of our Terms and Conditions.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time. On the Premium page, choose Manage subscription and cancel in the billing portal. Premium stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Cancelling stops future payments. It doesn't refund a payment you've already made.

Your first payment

If Premium isn't right for you, ask within 14 days of your first payment and we'll refund it in full. You don't need to give a reason.

Renewals

Monthly renewals aren't refunded once the new period has started, except in the cases below. To avoid the next charge, cancel before your renewal date.

Charges we always refund

  • A payment taken after you cancelled.
  • A second payment for the same period.
  • A payment you didn't authorise.

If SlapStat shuts down

If SlapStat is discontinued while you have an active Premium subscription, you will receive a refund for any unused portion of the period already paid for, unless otherwise required by law.

How to ask for a refund

Email info@slapstat.com from the address on your SlapStat account and tell us which payment you mean. You can also look up your order and contact Paddle at paddle.net.

How refunds are paid

Paddle returns the money to the payment method you used. How long it takes to show depends on your bank or card issuer.

When a payment is refunded, the Premium it paid for ends.

Your legal rights

Paddle's buyer terms and refund policy apply to Premium purchases, including any statutory consumer rights applicable to your purchase. Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under consumer law.

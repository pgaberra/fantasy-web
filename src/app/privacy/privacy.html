SlapStat is a fantasy hockey projection tool. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have.

Who is responsible

SlapStat is run by Alexander Berglund, an individual in Sweden, not a company. You can contact him at privacy@slapstat.com about your personal data or any of the rights described below.

What we store and why

Your account

We collect this when you create an account and keep it while your account exists:

  • Your email address, used to identify your account and send verification and password-reset emails.
  • Your password is stored securely and never in plain text. Social sign-ins use an account identifier provided by Google or Facebook instead.
  • Your internal account ID, whether your email is verified, and when the account was created.

We do not store your name, phone number, address, or date of birth. This information is enough to provide the account features you use, including saving your work and signing back in.

Your projections

The projections you create, including their name, season, player numbers, and scoring settings.

If you sync a projection from Yahoo, it also contains the league name and the team names of other managers in that league. Only you can see your saved projections, and you can delete them at any time.

Yahoo Fantasy (only if you connect it)

Connecting Yahoo is optional. If you connect it, we store your Yahoo access and refresh tokens in encrypted form, along with your Yahoo user ID, so we can read your league settings. League and team data is read from Yahoo as needed and is not stored unless you save it in a projection.

There is currently no disconnect button. Email us and we will remove the stored Yahoo connection.

Usage analytics (only if you agree)

If you accept the cookie banner, we record pages viewed and a small number of actions, such as registering, creating a projection, and starting a draft. While signed in, this data is linked to your account ID, not your email address.

If you decline, we still count the visit using a privacy-preserving hash generated by PostHog instead of a cookie. It is not linked to your account or other visits. You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Single-use tokens in password-reset and email-verification links are removed before analytics events leave your browser.

If you email us

Messages sent to info@slapstat.com or privacy@slapstat.com are stored while we handle your request and while the email thread remains in the mailbox. Ask us to delete a conversation and we will.

Companies your browser contacts directly

Some parts of the site load directly from third-party servers. Those companies receive your IP address and browser details as part of the request. We do not send them your account data, but the browser request itself cannot be hidden from them.

  • Google Fonts: the site loads our font from Google.
  • Google and Facebook: their sign-in code loads on the sign-in and registration pages.
  • Yahoo: player photos are served from Yahoo.
  • Paddle: its code loads on the pricing and checkout pages. The pricing page uses it to show the price in your local currency.

If you sign in with Google or Facebook, or connect Yahoo, those providers also process your data under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

Your account and projections stay until the account is deleted. Analytics data is kept for one year. Password-reset and email-verification records remain until you request another link or your account is deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask for a copy of your data, corrections, deletion, data portability, or that we stop a particular use of your data. Where we rely on consent for analytics, you can withdraw it at any time.

SlapStat does not yet have self-service delete or export controls. Email us and we will handle those requests manually.

If you believe we are handling your data incorrectly, you can complain to your local data protection authority. In Sweden, this is Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

Changes

If we change what we collect or who we share it with, we will update this policy and its date. Where a change requires your consent, we will ask for it.

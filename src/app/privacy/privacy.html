This Privacy Policy explains what personal data SlapStat collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how long it is retained.

Who is responsible

SlapStat is operated by Alexander Berglund, an individual based in Sweden.

For questions about your personal data or to exercise your rights, contact privacy@slapstat.com.

What data we collect

Account information

When you create a SlapStat account, we store:

  • Your email address, used to identify your account and send verification and password-reset emails.
  • A hash of your password, rather than the password itself. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we store an identifier provided by the relevant provider instead.
  • An internal account ID, your email verification status, and the date your account was created.

We do not store your name, phone number, physical address, or date of birth.

Projections

We store the projections you create, including:

  • Projection name
  • Season
  • Player selections
  • Scoring and projection settings

If you sync a projection from a Yahoo Fantasy league, it may also contain your league name and the team names of other managers in that league.

Your saved projections are private and can be deleted from your account.

Yahoo Fantasy

Connecting a Yahoo Fantasy account is optional.

If you connect your account, we store your Yahoo user ID and encrypted access and refresh tokens. These allow SlapStat to access your Yahoo Fantasy data when you use the integration.

League and team data is not stored separately, except for information that you choose to save as part of a projection.

SlapStat does not currently provide a self-service option to disconnect a Yahoo account. To disconnect your account, contact privacy@slapstat.com.

Analytics

If you accept analytics cookies, we use PostHog to collect information about how you use SlapStat, including pages visited and certain actions such as registering, creating a projection, or starting a draft.

When you are signed in, analytics events are linked to your internal account ID, but not to your email address.

If you decline analytics cookies, no analytics cookie is set. PostHog still receives basic usage information using a privacy-preserving identifier that is not linked to your account or used to recognise you across different days.

Authentication tokens in password-reset and email-verification links are removed before analytics data is sent.

Emails

If you contact us at info@slapstat.com or privacy@slapstat.com, we store your message and email address so that we can respond to you.

Messages are forwarded by Cloudflare and stored in a Google mailbox.

You can request deletion of an email conversation by contacting us.

Cookies and browser storage

SlapStat stores the following information in your browser:

Data Purpose When
Authentication tokens Keeps you signed in between visits When you sign in
Cookie preference Remembers your cookie preference When you respond to the cookie banner
PostHog analytics cookie Used for analytics Only if you accept analytics cookies

Authentication tokens contain your email address and should be treated as confidential.

Third-party services loaded by your browser

Some pages on SlapStat load content or functionality directly from third-party services. These services may receive your IP address and browser information when your browser connects to them.

These include:

  • Google Fonts, used to load fonts.
  • Google and Facebook, whose sign-in components are loaded on the sign-in and registration pages.
  • Yahoo, which hosts player images displayed on SlapStat.
  • Paddle, which is loaded on the pricing and checkout pages.

If you use Google, Facebook, or Yahoo integrations, those services may also process your data under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep your data

Your account and saved projections are retained until your account is deleted.

Analytics data is retained for one year.

Password-reset and email-verification links are single-use and expire after a short period. Records associated with used links may remain until another authentication request is made or your account is deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

SlapStat does not currently provide self-service options for account deletion or data export. To exercise these rights, contact privacy@slapstat.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Sweden, this is Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our data practices change.

The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated. Where required, we will ask for your consent before introducing changes that require it.

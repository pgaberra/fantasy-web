SlapStat is a fantasy hockey projection tool. This page explains exactly what we store about you, who else sees it, and what you can ask us to do about it. It describes what the software actually does — not what a template says it might.

What we store, and why

Your account

Created when you register, and kept for as long as your account exists:

  • Your email address — it identifies your account and is where we send verification and password-reset messages.
  • A hash of your password, never the password itself. If you signed up with Google or Facebook, there is no password at all — only an opaque account identifier from that provider.
  • An internal account ID, whether your email is verified, and when the account was created.

We store no name, phone number, address or date of birth. We rely on the above to give you the service you asked for: without an account we cannot save your work or let you sign back in.

Your projections

The projections you create — their name, the season, and the player numbers and scoring settings you entered.

If you sync a projection from a Yahoo league, it also saves your league's name and the team names of the other managers in it. Those names are often real names or nicknames, so a projection can contain information about people other than you. Only you can see your own projections, and you can delete any of them at any time.

Yahoo Fantasy — only if you connect it

Connecting your Yahoo account is entirely optional and nothing happens until you choose it. If you do, we store your Yahoo access and refresh tokens encrypted, along with your Yahoo user identifier, so we can read your league's scoring settings. Your league and team data itself is read from Yahoo as you use the app and is not stored, except for what you deliberately save into a projection.

There is no disconnect button yet. Once connected, those tokens stay until we remove them for you — email us and we will.

Usage analytics — only if you agree

If you accept the cookie banner, we record which pages you visit and a few actions (registering, creating a projection, starting a draft) so we can see what people use and where they get stuck. While signed in, this is linked to your account ID — never your email address.

If you decline, we still count the visit, but through a privacy-preserving hash generated on PostHog's servers rather than a cookie, and it is not linked to you or to any other day's visit. You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Links that carry a single-use token — password resets and email verification — have that token stripped out before any analytics event leaves your browser.

Companies your browser contacts directly

Some things on the page are loaded from other companies' servers. That means those companies receive your IP address and browser details simply because the page rendered — we never send them your account data, but we cannot hide the request from them either:

  • Google Fonts — our typeface loads from Google on every page, whether or not you have an account or accepted cookies.
  • Google and Facebook — their sign-in buttons load their code on the sign-in and registration pages, whether or not you use them.
  • Yahoo — player photos are served from Yahoo's servers wherever players are listed.

If you sign in with Google or Facebook, or connect Yahoo, those companies also handle your data under their own privacy policies — that relationship is between you and them.

How long we keep it

Your account and projections stay until the account is deleted. Analytics data is kept for one year. Password-reset and email-verification links stop working quickly and can only be used once, but the used-up record of one is not currently swept away on a schedule — it stays until you request another, or until we delete your account.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, hand it over in a portable form, or stop a particular use of it. Where we rely on your consent — that is, analytics — you can withdraw it at any time, and doing so is as easy as giving it.

SlapStat has no self-service delete or export button yet. Until it does, email the address above and we will do it by hand. We would rather say that plainly than imply a button exists.

If you think we are handling your data wrongly, you can complain to your local data protection authority. In Sweden that is Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

Changes

If we change what we collect or who we send it to, we will update this page and move the date at the top. Where a change needs your agreement, we will ask for it rather than assume it.

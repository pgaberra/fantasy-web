Questions

Can I cancel?
You can cancel anytime from your subscription page. Cancelling stops the next charge, and Premium remains active until the end of the period you already paid for.
Who charges my card?
Paddle sells SlapStat Premium on our behalf and handles payment and tax. Paddle is the name that appears on your card statement.
Can I get a refund?
Paddle handles refunds for Premium under its buyer terms, and statutory withdrawal rights apply where the law gives them. See the terms.
Will the price change?
The price you sign up at stays the same unless we tell you about a change in advance. See the terms.
